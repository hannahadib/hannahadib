Tamr · تمر

Learning Iraqi Arabic in my own words

Tamr is a mobile app for saving and practising Iraqi Arabic vocabulary. I’m half Iraqi, but I didn’t grow up speaking arabic. When I started taking lessons, I filled pages of notes with words and phrases. After every class, I’d find myself flicking through those pages looking for one word I knew I’d written down. It was frustrating, especially when I wanted to use a word in conversation.

​

Tamr makes studying Iraqi Arabic easier by keeping the words and phrases I learn in class together on my phone. I can find a word when I need it and practise wherever I am. Designed in Figma and built with AI, Tamr keeps the interface simple. I can save a phrase after class, look it up in my dictionary or review it with flashcards. It’s a practical companion to my lessons.
