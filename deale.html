Deale

A new digital identity for Spain’s first M&A platform

Deale connects business owners, investors and advisors looking to buy or sell a company. As the platform evolved and years went by, its website began to feel outdated. There was a clear need to change the way the website portrayed, making emphasis in showcasing investment opportunities and share the stories behind completed deals.

We redesigned the website and developed a visual identity that brings it closer to the product. We wanted the site to feel as modern as the technology behind Deale while retaining the sense of simpicity and professionalism.

Approach & solution

We built the homepage around a simple idea: visitors should be able to see what an opportunity on Deale looks like from the moment they arrive. Alongside the headline, we placed a grid of deal cards that pair company imagery with details such as location, revenue and EBITDA.

We paired deep navy typography with brighter blue accents, generous white space and a soft gradient behind the opening section. Large, direct headlines introduce each page, while smaller labels and figures sit in compact, neatly aligned groups. That shift in scale lets the site feel open and uncluttered, even when it presents a lot of financial information.
