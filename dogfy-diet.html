Dogfy Diet

A simpler way to manage dog’s food subscription

Dogfy Diet, Spain's leading natural-cooked dog food subscription service, aimed to re-design and enhance its customer area to improve user experience, increase engagement, and reduce reliance on customer support. Having been using the same platform for over 4 years, it started to feel outdated in looks and functionalities.

​

User feedback revealed frustrations with scattered action points and a lack of intuitive options, making simple tasks overly complicated. As a result, many customers resorted to contacting customer support, which increased operational costs and created unnecessary friction in the user journey. To address these issues, we embarked on a complete UX/UI redesign, focusing on modernizing the interface and implementing retention strategies to enhance customer satisfaction and engagement.

Approach & solution

  • Visual refresh of the old platform, where users could now adjust portion sizes, swap meal types, and change delivery schedules effortlessly, increasing satisfaction and reducing support tickets.
  • Simplified information architecture, consolidating actions into a single access point, eliminating the confusion of scattered options and focusing on training users to intuitively understand where and how to perform key actions, improving overall self-sufficiency.
  • Easier and more accessible customization. Users can now adjust portion sizes, swap meal plans, and change delivery schedules effortlessly and self-sufficiently, increasing satisfaction and reducing support tickets.
  • Optimized mobile experience with future scalability, designing with a mobile-first approach, ensuring the interface was fully responsive and intuitive on smaller screens.

Results

The redesign delivered measurable improvements in user engagement, self-service efficiency, and customer retention. The impact was significant with over 198,200 active subscribers in 6 months
